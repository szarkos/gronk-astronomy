## (e) Deep Sky Imaging Guide
- Workflow: confirm coordinates/visibility (Planning Tool or Telescopius), check framing, fill form, shortest sub-exposure with max SNR, darks 300 s Bin1&2 + bias, Acquire now or Create plan, watch System Status, download ~5 min after.

- Limits: 900 s max, 22° min elevation; suggested ≥45°.
