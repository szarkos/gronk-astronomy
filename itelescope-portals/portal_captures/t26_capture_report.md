## (e) Deep Sky Imaging Guide ("Deep Sky Imaging Instructions")
Workflow: Planning Tool or Telescopius for coordinates/visibility; check framing in Telescopius with correct telescope; fill Deep Sky Objects form, suggested H:M:S / D:M:S (RA 05:23:25 DEC -10:12:56); shortest sub-exposure for max S/N; master calibration Bias Bin 1 & 2, Darks 900 s Bin 1 & 2 (dark scaled with bias); Acquire images now or Create plan for later (Reservation); watch via System Status or View Desktop; download ~5 min after.
Tips: short subs reduce bad frames; stack many shorter subs (120 s x 10 = 1200 s master); sky noise just exceeding dark current is ideal; brighter object shorter sub, darker filter longer; wideband shorter than narrowband; match darks; dither each sub; prefer #REPEAT over Qty.
Notes: adaptive focus, disabled by #FORCESTARTUP + #NOADAPTIVE. Max 600 s, min elevation 15°, 45°+ suggested. Outputs: raw 16-bit FITS, calibrated 16-bit FITS, JPEG preview, optional 16-bit TIFF, optional calibrated FITS to VPhot.
