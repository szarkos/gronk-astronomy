## (e) Deep Sky Imaging Guide ("Deep Sky Imaging Instructions")
Workflow: use iTelescope Planning Tool or Telescopius to confirm coordinates/visibility; check framing in Telescopius with correct telescope (else object too small/large); fill Deep Sky Objects form, RA/Dec most formats, suggested H:M:S and D:M:S (e.g. RA 05:23:25, DEC -10:12:56); set subs to shortest time giving max S/N; Bias (Bin 1 and 2) and 1800 s darks (Bin 1 and 2) provided as masters, dark scaled with bias; click Acquire images now or Create plan for later (Reservation system); watch via System Status or View Desktop; images ready ~5 min after exposure.
Tips: short subs reduce bad frames from conditions and cosmic rays; stack many short subs (120 s x 10 vs one 1200 s, only marginally better in theory); ideal sub lets sky shot noise just exceed dark current; brighter objects shorter subs, darker filters longer, narrowband longer than wideband; match sub times to existing darks; dither each sub; prefer #REPEAT over Qty across filters (4 x LRGB 1200 s without #REPEAT cut at 3/4 gives L,R,G no B; #REPEAT 4 with 1 per filter gives complete sets).
Notes: adaptive focus periodically and on temperature change, disabled by #FORCESTARTUP + #NOADAPTIVE. Limits 1800 s max, 10° min elevation, 45°+ suggested. Each request produces raw 16-bit FITS, calibrated 16-bit FITS, JPEG preview, optional 16-bit TIFF, optional calibrated FITS to VPhot.
